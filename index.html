        How to read this view

        A day is a gauge

        Each dial runs from 8 am at the lower left, over the top at noon, to 5 pm at the lower right. The number in the middle is that day's teaching hours. On today's dial, the dark needle marks the time now.

        Read the gaps

        Grey stretches of the ring are free time. The line under each dial names the longest free window between 8 am and 5 pm.

        Colour means subject

        Blue is Research and green is CHN-I. A dashed, faded arc is an unconfirmed class on a day marked as holiday or leave.
